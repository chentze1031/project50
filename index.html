Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Project 50 ("the App") is a 50-day habit-building app. Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what data the App collects, how it is used, and your choices.

1. Data We Collect

Data you provide: When you create a challenge, the App stores your difficulty setting, daily rule completion status, streak history, and personalization choices (alarm time, book title, workout type). If you sign in with Google, we store your account identifier and email address for authentication.

Data collected automatically: We use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage events (app opens, challenge starts and completions, feature usage) and Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash reports. These include device model, OS version, and app version — no personal content.

If you use the App as a guest, your data stays on your device and is not uploaded.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To operate and improve the App
  • To sync your challenge progress across devices (when signed in)
  • To diagnose crashes and fix bugs
  • To understand aggregate usage patterns

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share data only with:

  • Firebase (Google) — analytics and crash reporting
  • Supabase — cloud database and authentication

Both operate under their own privacy policies and process data on our behalf.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Your challenge data is kept as long as your account exists. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings → Account → Delete account inside the App; deletion is immediate and irreversible. Alternatively, email chentze961031@gmail.com from the address linked to your account and we will process the request within 30 days. Anonymous analytics are retained for 14 months per Google's default retention.

5. Your Rights

You can request a copy of your data, correct inaccuracies, or request deletion by contacting us. If you are in the EU/EEA, you have rights under GDPR including data portability and the right to object to processing.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If we learn we have, we will delete it promptly.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be communicated via an in-app notice. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email chentze961031@gmail.com.

隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 9 月 10 日

Project 50（下称「本应用」）是一款 50 天习惯养成应用。我们重视你的隐私。本政策说明本应用收集哪些数据、如何使用，以及你拥有的选择权。

1. 我们收集的数据

你提供的数据：当你创建挑战时，本应用会存储你的难度设置、每日规则完成状态、连续记录历史，以及个性化选项（闹钟时间、书名、运动类型）。若你使用 Google 登录，我们会存储你的账号标识和邮箱地址用于身份验证。

自动收集的数据：我们使用 Firebase Analytics 收集匿名使用事件（应用启动、挑战开始与完成、功能使用情况），并使用 Firebase Crashlytics 收集崩溃报告。这些数据包含设备型号、系统版本和应用版本，不包含任何个人内容。

若你以访客身份使用，数据仅保存在你的设备本地，不会上传。

2. 我们如何使用数据

  • 运营和改进本应用
  • 在你登录后跨设备同步挑战进度
  • 诊断崩溃并修复缺陷
  • 了解整体使用模式

3. 数据共享

我们不出售你的数据。我们仅与以下服务方共享数据：

  • Firebase（Google） — 用于分析和崩溃上报
  • Supabase — 用于云端数据库和身份验证

两者均遵循各自的隐私政策，并代表我们处理数据。

4. 数据保留与删除

你的挑战数据将在账号存续期间保留。你可以随时在应用内通过 设置 → 账户 → 删除账号 永久删除账号及全部相关数据，删除立即生效且不可恢复。你也可以使用账号绑定的邮箱发送邮件至 chentze961031@gmail.com，我们将在 30 天内处理。匿名分析数据按 Google 默认策略保留 14 个月。

5. 你的权利

你可以联系我们索取数据副本、更正错误信息或要求删除。若你位于欧盟／欧洲经济区，你享有 GDPR 规定的权利，包括数据可携权和拒绝处理权。

6. 儿童隐私

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童。我们不会在知情的情况下收集 13 岁以下儿童的数据。如发现此类情况，我们将立即删除。

7. 政策变更

我们可能更新本政策。重大变更将通过应用内通知告知。变更后继续使用即视为接受。

8. 联系我们

对本政策有疑问？请发邮件至 chentze961031@gmail.com。
